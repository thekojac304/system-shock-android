ARCHITECTURE INVESTIGATION

1. Selection is by tile music zone, not a deck-to-file table. musicai.c:
   load_score_for_location reads me_bits_music; cyberspace substitutes its
   score base. fade_into_location changes the theme when the tile zone
   changes, with special handling for continuous elevator music.
   Medical is player_struct.level == 1 (player.c:init_player). HOSPITAL_ZONE
   is also 1, but zone and deck are distinct concepts. AUDIO1 uses the deck.

2. musicai.c:load_score_guts calls musicai_shutdown then MacTuneLoadTheme.
   SDLSound.c:MacTuneLoadTheme stops XMI, frees the previous XMI, loads
   res/sound/<sblaster or genmidi>/thmN.xmi and res/sound/thmN.bin. Named
   title/end themes use titloop.xmi/endloop.xmi instead of the theme tables.

3. airupt.c:grind_music_ai/check_asynch_ai and musicai.c:mlimbs_do_ai decide
   pieces/layers. make_request and MacTuneStartCurrentTheme call StartTrack.
   Xmi.c parses/sequences events on its worker, uses atomic track commands
   and a synth mutex, and implements StartTrack, StopTrack, StopTheMusic,
   InitReadXMI, ReloadDecXMI, ShutdownReadXMI and UpdateVolumeXMI.
   Many historical MacTune functions, including MacTuneQueueTune and
   MacTuneShutdown, are now stubs: do not assume their names imply playback.

4. MusicDevice.c provides ADLMIDI (Android's default OPL synthesis), native
   MIDI (Windows/ALSA when available), optional FluidSynth, and a null device.
   Android disables FluidSynth and SDL_mixer MIDI; the engine owns MIDI.
   Xmi.c:MusicCallback renders the synth through Mix_HookMusic.

5. SDLSound.c:snd_start_digital owns the Android mixer device: 48 kHz,
   signed 16-bit stereo, 1024 frames. VOC/SFX use Mix_Chunk channels.
   The Android auxiliary postmix drains cutscene_audiostream for movies/
   audiologs. Desktop retains its separate raw SDL auxiliary-device path.
   The Android SDL build includes OpenSL ES, AAudio and Java AudioTrack;
   actual device driver selection requires hardware logs.

6. AndroidProject/app/jni/CMakeLists.txt explicitly sets SDL2MIXER_VORBIS="".
   OGG/Vorbis is NOT supported in this build. MP3/minimp3 and WAVE are on.
   SDL_mixer 2.8.1's minimp3 CreateFromRW uses read/seek callbacks and a
   bounded PCM buffer. It may scan/index the compressed file when opening;
   it does not decode a whole long soundtrack into an in-memory PCM chunk.
   Mix_LoadMUS/Mix_PlayMusic are the existing suitable streaming facility.

7. Existing lifecycle, and AUDIO1 treatment:
   - New game: init_player starts level 1; gamewrap.c loads the location
     theme. AUDIO1 starts on the first eligible music update in gameplay.
   - Load: gamewrap.c:load_game reads the saved level then reloads location
     music. AUDIO1 checks that actual level; saves contain no AUDIO1 state.
   - Deck transition: saveload.c retains elevator music in some paths;
     the first destination music update releases AUDIO1 even when no new
     tile theme load occurred. Stock requests then play the current theme.
   - In-game pause: gameloop.c explicitly keeps calling mlimbs_do_ai.
     Stock music continues; AUDIO1 also continues, with no new play call.
   - Background/foreground: SDLActivity/native pause semaphores and SDL's
     blocking Android event pump pause/resume backend devices. The mixer
     and its music object survive. No AUDIO1 Java/event resume handler or
     second audio device was added. Process death naturally starts fresh.
   - Death: damage.c:player_dies calls mai_player_death, which stops music
     and requests the stock death transition. AUDIO1 suspends its stream;
     player_struct.dead excludes replacement playback. Resurrection resumes
     the retained stream on the next eligible Medical update.
   - Menu/cutscene: setup.c/cutsloop.c stop the theme. AUDIO1 suspends and
     restores the synth hook. Menu/death/cutscene music remains stock.
     GAME_LOOP, FULLSCREEN_LOOP and AUTOMAP_LOOP are eligible; other loops
     cannot activate the replacement through tight_loop calls.
   - Exit: AUDIO1 registers cleanup after the sound system's atexit handlers,
     so its stream is halted/freed before mixer teardown. Restart rereads
     prefs and starts from the beginning, with no saved decoder position.

8. Future AUDIO3 hooks: WALKING_SCORE, PERIL_SCORE, COMBAT_SCORE;
   mai_attack/mai_combat_length/mlimbs_combat; mai_monster_nearby and defeated;
   in_peril/mlimbs_peril; health and accumulated damage/layer_danger;
   success, machine and transition layers; track/transition/layer/key tables.
   These are music heuristics, not a normalized intensity API. AUDIO1 skips
   stock music sequencing while overriding (but still flushes damage SFX).
   A future dynamic replacement controller must deliberately consume/update
   these signals; it should not assume that the stock sequencer keeps running.

9. Clean interception: ExternalMusic.c owns only external music and the
   music-hook swap. mlimbs_do_ai supplies level/context/enable/volume.
   StopTheMusic suspends the override; StartTrack excludes concurrent stock
   MIDI; UpdateVolumeXMI forwards music-volume/audiolog ducking changes.
   No mixer channel, postmix callback or device lifecycle is replaced.

10. Storage: LauncherActivity imports owned data to getFilesDir()/res.
    Shock.c changes cwd to SDL_AndroidGetInternalStoragePath (app files/).
    Prefs.c prefers cwd/prefs.txt then SDL_GetPrefPath; on Android the latter
    is the same internal files directory. There is no general public-folder
    music importer today. Use QA run-as/ADB for this developer prototype.

DESIGN AND LIMITS

Only mapping: level 1 -> music/remake/medical.mp3, including Medical's
elevator while still on level 1. Every other deck and cyberspace remain stock.
Configuration: replacement_medical_music = true/false in prefs.txt.
Default false. Recognized true values follow existing prefs: true, yes, 1.
Normal music must also be enabled (music-volume greater than zero).

The decoder is opened before suppressing stock music, and looped with -1.
Repeated updates never call PlayMusic again. Theme reloads suspend/resume
the same object; departure/disable frees it. No crossfade is implemented.
Load, unsupported-type and play failures restore/retain the stock callback;
unexpected stream termination also falls back on the next update. One
concise AUDIO1 fallback diagnostic is emitted per attempt; failed attempts
are latched until deck departure, disable or process restart to avoid spam.
Adding/fixing a file after failure therefore requires one of those actions.

Only MP3 is accepted by this prototype, even if a desktop mixer supports
other formats. Renaming OGG/WAV to .mp3 does not make it supported. The fixed
MP3 choice reuses the current decoder and avoids editing the already-dirty
Android build configuration. OGG/Vorbis can be enabled deliberately later.
MP3 encoder padding may produce a small loop gap; assess the supplied track.

Volume follows the existing independent music volume, including audiolog
ducking; SFX and speech volume controls are unchanged. A separate pack-volume
preference is deferred. Restoring the synth hook restores its established
full mixer gain; stock volume remains controlled by MIDI controllers.

The module is portable C with no Android APIs. A no-SDL_mixer build uses
no-op stubs. Both primary and legacy 32-bit CMake source lists include it.
AUDIO2 can replace the one mapping with a pack lookup without changing
playback ownership. AUDIO3 simultaneous ambient/combat streams will need a
deliberate multi-stream mixing design: SDL_mixer's one music slot is enough
for AUDIO1, not a complete crossfade/layer engine.
