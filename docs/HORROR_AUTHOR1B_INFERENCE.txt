2. REUSABLE INFERENCE CHANGES
frtables.c face_obstruct is parsed fail-closed as the renderer's authoritative
edge-width table. The edge calculation combines partial-face widths, map floor
and ceiling heights, slope parameter and mirror mode following frterr.c's
edge_get_fandc / mmask_facelet semantics. Regions use multiple edge samples,
not a single center-wide rectangular slab. Receiver rays check the actual
crossing point and its vertical open interval. Diagonal corners still fail
closed. Internal complex tile walls are not fully modeled.

Boundary-mounted door objects are located from their sub-tile position. When
the position and surrounding aperture identify an edge, that edge is a closed
default portal and its door-frame runtime gate, object ID, other-half ID,
heading and confidence are retained in diagnostics. Whole-cell blocking remains
for ambiguous interior doors. This removes false isolation on 58/63 Medical
and 54/58 Executive boundary doors. Physical panel width, handedness and exact
opening animation are unverified; an open portal is a representation, not an
offline assertion that the door is currently open. No light crosses it by
default. The region graph distinguishes portal, blocked geometry and visual
region split edges. Visual splits can transmit when a ray confirms geometry.

Regions grow across verified optical apertures with local darkness and height
continuity, hazard/music continuity and a bounded footprint. This is visual
receiving topology, not pathfinding or semantic room naming. It reduced but
did not eliminate fragmentation. Tile masks now use five target samples and
edge-height ray checks, avoiding many center-only false negatives. They can
still include a partially hidden face, so they are diagnostics rather than a
fragment-level shading guarantee. U-shaped walls, corner ties and closed doors
are excluded in focused tests.

Shared rules now classify each material surface as emits-normal, emits-low,
emissive-only or review-only. The neutral material record carries all three
surface policies, while actual whole-texture emissive stays disabled pending
pixel masks. Known luminous ceiling textures may emit from sloped surface height, inset
from the surface. Reviewed 35/36 floor strips and a limited set of exposed
35/36/20/82 wall assignments emit low energy. Unsupported luminous faces remain
review-only. Generic wall assignments are checked for an exposed closed edge,
though face identity remains uncertain. Named object lamps at an exact ceiling
anchor are inset below it rather than rejected as out of bounds; all six
Executive chandeliers now generate lights. Positions and RGB remain design
approximations in map-height units, never meters.

WORDS_TRIPLE 0x70203 with TEXBITMAP render_type 8 is a distinct display
candidate. Objects 699 and 718 are found, but remain emissive-review; text
bitmap alone is insufficient evidence for a physical local light. Generic
text bitmap objects are not promoted. This avoids ordinary signage casting.

No whole mixed texture is marked emissive. Luminous catalog materials carry
pixel_mask_review and are non-emissive until reviewed source palette-index
masks can distinguish bright elements from dark housing and track palette
cycling. This policy preserves original art and avoids a global bright-pixel
threshold. Reviewed masks themselves were not created in this phase, so
runtime emissive rendering is an unresolved material task.

Repeated same-resource candidates within 1.4 map units cluster to one source.
Generated receivers have at most four local lights and 1.8 aggregate source
intensity budget; low floor/wall sources contribute 28% of ceiling base before
the original darkness response. Saturated receivers are excluded or the next
source is suppressed. This bounds proposed energy without globally lifting
original darkness. It is not an image-space luminance guarantee. Original
floor/ceiling darkness and live transient lighting remain authoritative.

5. EXECUTIVE TRANSFER
The same rules reduce 405/271 regions/singletons to 225/81. All six named
chandeliers (62, 63, 69, 70, 180, 181) now generate inset object sources.
Repeated ceiling panels create 113 clustered suppressions. 288 of 506 tiles
whose two original darkness channels are >=10 remain without a proposed local
light, down from 394; no deck-wide ambient lift was added. Wall/door leakage
is improved at sampled rays but not validated in rendered gameplay. No
Executive room-specific exception or manual light was introduced.

6. DIAGNOSTICS AND TESTS
The generator emits region, independent original-darkness, fixtures, materials,
hazard/state, receiver, portal, suppressed, cluster and emissive-status SVGs
for each level. The review script also emits Medical's dedicated held-out PNG.
Generated JSON includes exact source candidates, policy/reasons, door portals,
region graph, masks and source data. All images and generated profiles stay in
ignored dist/. No screenshots, save data or proprietary assets are tracked.

PASS: 14 focused unittests cover edge apertures, partial/diagonal exclusion,
height separation, closed boundary door and open geometry representation,
ambiguous whole-cell door, U-shaped and corner leakage, slope placement,
low floor/wall emission, text-bitmap positive/negative cases, mixed-emissive
non-glow, repeated-fixture clustering, receiver energy budget, deterministic
generation, schema rejection and manual override survival. The two-level
test-horror-author.py gate passes byte-identical regeneration, overlay
preservation, every receiver's five-sample visibility check, and unchanged
input hashes. Generated host times were about 0.25s Medical and 0.15s
Executive in this run. These are offline host measurements, not frame times.

7. REMAINING FAILURES, HUMAN WORK AND GATE
False positives: a luminous wall resource may be assigned to a blocked edge
without proving that its luminous subpanel actually faces the receiver;
five-sample tile inclusion can light hidden fragments; floor strips may cast
too widely; door extent and animation are unknown. False negatives: some
partial interior geometry and off-center apertures still fail closed; 1438
and 1502 remain uncovered in the suite; unreviewed text displays do not cast;
mixed-material emitters have no accepted pixel masks. Candidate colors,
radius and intensity are seeds, not recovered photometry.

Planning estimate revised from AUTHOR1's 60-75% automatic ordinary-area setup
to roughly 65-75% automatic setup and 25-35% substantive manual/rule review,
LOW confidence from two levels only. Every area still requires visual review.
Human art direction remains necessary for warm/cold story contrast, signature
reveals, emissive pixel masks, fog, named places, dramatic focal balance,
navigation/readability and plot-state color. The likely workflow remains
generate -> review -> improve shared rules -> regenerate -> targeted tweak,
not room-by-room manual setup.

The offline structural gate passes provisionally because the held-out suite
now has independently inferred local light and Executive improves under the
same rules. The runtime integration gate fails: door panel extent/state,
fragment-safe receiver masks and reviewed emissive masks are incomplete.
No GLES profile loader, selector, QA overlay, device comparison or performance
measurement was entered. GPU-RENDER6 and full-station authoring were not begun.
Recommended next phase: validate exact door panel extents/heading and surface
receiver faces, author reusable source-index emissive masks for a small fixture
set, then re-run the held-out gate before a bounded GLES review.
