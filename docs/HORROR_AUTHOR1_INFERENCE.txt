overlay application, resource-ID material catalog, whole-level diagnostic maps,

3. SIGNAL INVENTORY AND CONFIDENCE

Confidence concerns visual intent, not merely whether a field can be read.
STRONG means a direct semantic or spatial fact within its stated scope.
MODERATE means a useful reusable heuristic. WEAK means review-only evidence.
UNSAFE means do not turn that fact alone into atmosphere or an emitting light.

Signal                           Class / actual meaning and restriction
-------------------------------- -----------------------------------------------
Floor darkness                   STRONG original authored brightness, 0..15.
Ceiling darkness                 STRONG independent authored channel, 0..15.
Transient floor/ceiling light    STRONG live scalar contribution; not RGB intent.
Lighting traps/events            STRONG spatial/state scalar changes; color unknown.
Texture resource ID              STRONG identity; MODERATE material semantics after
                                 one-time catalog review. Resolve level-local slots.
Texture family byte              WEAK/UNSAFE as semantic taxonomy. See section 6.
Wall/floor/ceiling assignments   STRONG assignment; WEAK proof of exposed surface.
Animated texture group/position STRONG animation linkage; WEAK luminous intent.

                                 for reviewed fixtures on supported surfaces.
Monitors/displays                STRONG render exemption where source says display;
                                 MODERATE visible-emission candidate, not a lamp.
Light-looking fixtures           STRONG for named lamp object class; MODERATE for
                                 reviewed texture; bulb position still approximate.

4. SOURCE CONTRACTS THAT MATTER

Paths below are under SourceSnapshot/src/GameSrc unless otherwise stated.
archiveformat.c: FullMapLayout (58 bytes), MapElemLayout (16 bytes), ObjV11Layout
(27 bytes), ObjV12Layout (28 bytes), FixtureLayout (30), DoorLayout (14), and
TrapLayout (28) are the on-disk authority. Map resources are 4000+100*level:
  +2 version; +4 FullMap; +5 tiles; +7 texture-ID table; +8 objects;
  +19 fixtures; +20 doors; +22 traps; +42 texture animation state.

Headers/map.h, mapflags.h: t[10]&15 floor darkness, t[11]&15 ceiling darkness;
t[15] packs low-nibble floor/high-nibble ceiling transient light. Floor/ceiling
height bytes each have a distinct high hazard bit: bio and radiation. flag2 bit4
is PERIL, not radiation. Its upper three bits are the music zone. Debug render
and seen flags are not material or atmosphere labels.

Map Z = map-height step / 2^z_shift; object Z = raw byte / 2^(3+z_shift).
Both validation maps have z_shift=3. X/Y object positions are 8.8 map units.
The profile uses one common positive-Z map coordinate system, not meters.

frterr.c: edge_get_fandc/map_get_edge, face_obstruct and mmask_facelet are the
correct next source of precise edge apertures. A generic four-neighbor flood fill
is not equivalent. _fr_do_light_val and current transient/lamp/global behavior
must remain authoritative for live scalar light during later integration.

textmaps.c/Headers/textmaps.h: version9 TEXTPROP has 363 packed 11-byte records.
There is no authored RGB, luminous flag, physical radius or material-name field.
objprop.c numtypes_array defines 476 object triples; objsim.c:1856 loads their
27-byte common property records at EOF. The diagnostic accepts the inspected
original v45/17951-byte layout only. Render_type and LIGHT_TYPE are read exactly;
other layouts fail closed. It does not guess class-property sizes.

objsim.c:obj_is_display recognizes TEXTPOLY plus named screen triples.
gameobj.c and Headers/objbit.h define LIGHT_TYPE and conditional UNLIT behavior.
Headers/otrip.h names desk lamp, floor lamp, glow bulb and chandelier triples.
Not all things previously described informally as displays use obj_is_display.

5. REGION GRAPH AND SEGMENTATION

Implemented, deterministic, conservative:
  1. Enumerate all non-solid tiles. Doors become blocked portal-cell candidates.
  2. Rectangular open tiles and slope types6..21 may connect only when their
     conservative vertical slabs overlap. Diagonal footprints are quarantined.
     Slope slab uses the maximum possible floor and minimum possible ceiling,
     honoring MAP_FFLAT/MAP_CFLAT. A closed slab is unresolved, not a solid fact.
  3. Grow regions with matching hazard tuple and music category. Relative to
     each deterministic seed, require darkness-channel differences <=4,
     floor difference <=.75 tile, ceiling difference <=1 tile and X/Y offsets
     <=12 tiles. These are heuristics, explicitly not semantic room definitions.
  4. Name IDs from level + minimum seed tile. Label portal/elevator/hazard/
     uncertain transition first, then corridor/alcove/hall/room candidates by
     size and bounding shape. Class labels remain hypotheses.
  5. Emit adjacency edges labelled door, heuristic split or unverified geometry.
     Every emitted inter-region edge has light_transmission=false in this version.

This avoids treating an entire connected deck as one visual region. It still
over-segments: Medical 592 regions (413 singletons), Executive 405 (271 singletons).
They are conservative authoring cells, not 592/405 recovered semantic rooms.
Medical has only 2 hall candidates; Executive 3. See exact class counts in review.
The largest regions contain 31 and 48 tiles. 65.12% and 77.91% of tiles belong
to multi-tile regions. Those percentages measure grouping, NOT correct room labels.

Reliable now: separating solid gaps, conservative door cells, hazard categories,
elevator hints, supported rectangular spaces. Ambiguous: slopes whose narrow
open edges exceed the common slab, diagonal edges, intentional alcoves, thin
doors on cell boundaries, long/bent rooms, partial walls and multiple stories.
Doors at x/y just below an integer are particularly poor whole-cell separators.
The graph is optical/authoring evidence, not pathfinding or proof of accessibility.

No texture-change hard cuts: repeated panels otherwise produce one region per
material change. Composition, fixture count and lighting statistics are region
features for review. No claimed architecture interprets every change as a room.

Later overrides can replace masks/names, split or merge regions using a complete
transaction of exact-ID replacements and updated light references. Current
validator requires a disjoint complete base partition. Priority is reserved for
future overlay volumes; overlapping base region masks are rejected today.
New semantic names and arbitrary manual light insertion are future extensions,
not silently supported by the present replace/delete patch format.

7. FIXTURE CONFIDENCE AND LOCAL-LIGHT GENERATION

Candidate record keeps source texture/object ID, tile, surface, approximate XYZ,
RGB seed, classification, confidence, rejection reason and generation policy.

HIGH .90: reviewed luminous resource on an ordinary flat ceiling, not sky.
HIGH .85: named class7/subclass3 lamp, valid location inside an ordinary flat cell.
MODERATE .55: wall/floor luminous assignment, display, or unsupported placement.
LOW/UNKNOWN: arbitrary bright signs/paint/unknown IDs; no automatic local light.
These scores are rule tiers, not calibrated probabilities. Parameter confidence
for every generated light is only .45, regardless of source detection confidence.

Ceiling position = tile center, ceiling Z-.08. Named objects use their stored
location; this is NOT yet a verified bulb center. Generic lamp RGB=(1,.85,.65).
Texture light RGB is a reusable material-catalog seed estimated from original art.
Ceiling radius2.5, object radius2.0 map units; base intensity1.5/1.0.
Intensity is multiplied by .35+.65*(1-(floorDark+ceilingDark)/32), preserving a
monotone response to original authored darkness. These are design seeds, not
recovered photometry or gameplay lighting values. Falloff=max(0,1-distance/r)^2.

Suppress nearby fixture candidates within1.75 units inside one receiver region;
do not stack a new full-strength light for every adjacent ceiling panel. Cap at
four selected local lights per receiver tile; cap violations suppress the entire
candidate so selection remains deterministic. This produces184/130 whole-level
records, not a184/130-light shader loop. Future runtime loads per-region small lists.
Detection and suppression remain visible in diagnostics rather than disappearing.

8. RECEIVERS AND LEAKAGE

Radius alone is never sufficient. Current generated receiver mask is constrained
to the source's own region and to flat cells with a clear conservative line to the
receiver cell center at its mid-height. A grid-boundary line test checks both
adjacent cells at corner ties, rejecting blocked cells and height intersections.
It rejects a U-shaped same-region shortcut through a wall. Door portal cells do
not generate/receive local lights. Cross-door spill is disabled even if open.

TEST: walls, closed-door cells, corner ties, height occlusion and bent same-region
spaces are covered. Every actual emitted receiver center passed the same rule.
This proves a bounded static CENTER mask, not fragment-level absence of leaks.
A partially occluded tile may contain both visible and hidden fragments. Object
occluders, moving floors, changed terrain and exact portal apertures require
dynamic invalidation and finer masks before integration. Do not load these masks
as a claim of complete wall-leak prevention. Runtime light propagation is absent.

Next: derive exact floor/ceiling edge apertures from the existing terrain tables;
generate subcell/face receiver masks or conservatively split affected draws;
gate confirmed door portals on live door state. A single connected-region bit
cannot solve light around corners. No shadow maps are needed for this next step.

9. LEVEL ARCHETYPES AND AMBIENT

Hand-author a small number of level archetypes, then infer local variation. Do not
algorithmically invent a deck's emotional identity from average texture hue.
Only Medical and Executive archetypes are instantiated here:
  Medical cold_clinical: ambient RGB(.72,.88,1), intensity.16.
  Executive warm_neutral: RGB(1,.88,.76), intensity.16.
They are deliberately marked as design seeds. No uniform final color grade is
applied. Original material color remains the source albedo; grading defaults to
saturation1/exposure0. Catalog-local RGB fixtures supply contrasting accents.

Recommended later archetype family, DESIGN only:
  Medical cold clinical; Research cold machinery; Maintenance olive/ochre service;
  Storage sparse industrial; Reactor warm danger; Executive warmer neutral;
  Engineering harsh machinery; Security/Bridge hostile alert;
  Groves separate vegetation/organic treatment. Recovery is not a universal
  named room class; infer its fixtures, then name important locations manually.

Proposed diffuse illumination:
  A = ambientRGB * ambientIntensity * pow(clamp(1-Dlive/16,0,1), exponent)
  L = sum(receiverAllowed * RGB * intensity * squaredLinearFalloff)
  worldLinear = originalLinearRGB * (A + L)
  apply reviewed emissive policy, then optional region distance haze;
  encode display gamma once through the existing GLES presentation contract.
Exponent2, ambient floor0 here. There is no new blanket darkness offset and no
16-row lookup requirement. Original floor/ceiling spatial variation remains data;
runtime Dlive includes the original transient, lamp, muzzle and global effects.
Do not apply this curve twice to already-converted scalar lighting. Reuse the
GPU-RENDER1B contract rather than transplanting indexed bank IDs or HvLightPool.
Navigation/readability with these new seeds has NOT been visually tested.

10. FOG / COLORED HAZE

Geometry can suggest depth opportunities, not the presence of particles. Long
corridors, large spaces and high ceilings are MODERATE compositional hints;
hazards, damage, low light, industrial textures and organic textures are WEAK
evidence for actual haze. Radiation is not necessarily visible gas. Music-zone
PARK is also used in access corridors. Do not apply green fog because a tile is
flagged hazardous or green-colored.

Generator emits disabled review suggestions for hall/corridor/hazard regions.
Default fog and every regional suggestion are enabled=false, density0. No fog
appears automatically on either deck. Proposed Medical RGB(.12,.20,.24), Executive
(.18,.16,.14), start4/end16; these are optional design seeds, not observed mist.

Schema carries enabled, RGB, model(exp/linear), density, start/end, optional
height base/falloff and state channel. First runtime implementation should use
simple truecolor distance haze, with exp alpha=1-exp(-density*max(0,d-start)),
or a linear interval. Integrate along the relevant region segment, not by tinting
the entire view based on the camera's current room. Height influence is reserved;
no volumetrics. Keep emissive readability and UI separation explicit.

Verdict: automatic fog CANDIDATES are useful; actual fog should mostly be
archetype/manual-approved, sparse and reviewed across portals.

11. DYNAMIC-STATE INVENTORY

EASY existing hooks, no new saved state:
  - Live map permanent/transient scalar fields: already include authored changes.
  - Active/source object identity and runtime transform; disappearance can remove
    an explicitly associated lamp. HP alone cannot establish lamp destruction.
  - Door current_frame/instance blocking state for an explicit confirmed portal;
    locked/access level is not the same as closed/open or power off/on.
  - Known trap lighting state: trigger.c trap_functions index7 calls
    trap_main_light_func and trap_lighting_func. p1 packs two qdata operands;
    normal modes resolve endpoint objects, radial mode uses a radius.
    p2 low16 transition type, bits16..27 transition step, high nibble state;
    p3 selects mode0 plain,1 east/west,2 north/south,3 radial and floor/ceiling
    channel bits0x10000/0x20000. p4 contains four lighting target bytes.
    Eight scheduled steps implement existing transitions. Observe them; never
    create an independent contradictory atmosphere save-state machine.

DATA: Medical16 lighting events, all16 direct bounds resolved offline;
Executive21,20 bounds resolved,1 retained unresolved rather than guessed.
Decoded params/bounds/state appear in review/review.json. Bounds are affected
scalar areas, not automatic physical emitter locations. Arbitrary comparators
and chained activation conditions are preserved as raw known data for resolution.

POSSIBLE hooks, require explicit effect mappings:
  - Quest trap index4; comparator_check/qdata_get use actual bit/variable IDs.
    qdata0x1000 selects a variable,0x2000 a bit; untagged low12 is a constant.
    Do not evaluate quest references using the generator's host-side guesses.
  - SHODAN_QV=0x10+level (Headers/shodan.h), normalized by qdata_get using
    initial_shodan_vals and mission difficulty. This is security/shodometer state,

  - Terrain/height/texture traps, object transmogrification/destruction and

    original game performs the operation. Do not replay the trap for visuals.
  - tpolys.h SHODAN_STATIC_MAGIC_COOKIE0x76 and existing SHODAN render effects:
    preserve special-view precedence; not a new universal region label.

MANUAL mapping required: generic power restored/disabled, alarm, named plot-room
transitions, local reactor glow, authored corruption progression, damaged area

Medical also inventories17 height,10 questbit and12 transmogrify events;

12. NEUTRAL PROFILE CONTRACT

Machine-readable contract: profiles/horror-author1/profile.schema.json.
LevelProfile: version, generator provenance, level ID, baseline/rules/texture/
object-property signatures, coordinate/color units, archetype, ambient, darkness
response, fog and grading, region/light/material/dynamic collections.
Region: stable ID/name, origin, explicit tile mask, height bounds, priority,
classification/confidence, ambient/fog/grading overrides and diagnostic statistics.
Light: stable source ID, XYZ, RGB, intensity, radius/falloff, receiver region IDs
AND receiver tile mask, state predicate, source resource/object ID, source
confidence, separate parameter confidence, generated/manual provenance.
MaterialRule: resource ID, raw family/animation references, classification,
emissive proposal/gain, original-art grading policy, local-light policy,
special handling and generated/manual provenance.
DynamicOverride: known-state predicate, target IDs, presentation values,
transition duration/curve and provenance. Its existence does not imply a hook
has been connected to runtime yet.

No palette-bank number, 16-row output index, CLUT pointer, GPU handle, packed
receiver uniform index, or shader implementation detail is an art-direction field.
Diagnostics are an explicitly separate non-runtime section. A future runtime
compiler should strip them, build tile-to-region arrays and bounded light lists,
and reject unresolved special_handling rather than silently treating it as done.

Validation: strict v1 shape/ranges, unknown fields/enums rejected, finite numbers,
unique IDs, complete disjoint tile coverage, valid receiver references/membership,
four-light receiver budget, valid dynamic targets and fog intervals. A small
dependency-free checker implements exactly the keywords in the shipped standard
JSON Schema; adding an unsupported validation keyword fails closed. It does not
accept external schemas or claim to be a general-purpose JSON Schema engine.

Signatures bind to baseline archive resource blocks + texture/object properties
+ generator rules. Baseline signature includes the ORIGINAL resource snapshot,
not current mutable save bytes. At runtime validate the installed baseline data
once, identify its compatible level, then observe live state separately. Hashing
a live save including seen bits/transients would incorrectly reject legitimate
play. Object IDs are level-and-baseline-qualified, not global permanent identities.

Unknown profile version, missing resource, stale overlay, invalid mask or unsupported
geometry => reject profile application. Proposed runtime fallback is the accepted
neutral GLES scalar behavior and existing whole-view software fallback when its
renderer guard requires it. Never use partial unchecked profile data or alter
gameplay because a cosmetic profile fails validation. Runtime loader remains design.

13. GENERATED / MANUAL LAYERS

Generator owns level-N.generated.json only. Human overrides live in a different
file containing schema_version, baseline_signature and patches. Each patch has
collection, exact stable id, and a complete replacement record, or null to delete.
Replacement origin becomes manual. Stale/missing/ambiguous IDs and incompatible
baseline signatures produce an error; never silently drop or retarget art direction.
Use --overlay <path> with exactly one --levels value to emit a separate merged
file. The original generated file remains unchanged by override application.
Generator refuses output paths that would overwrite its inputs or overlay.

Regeneration recomputes generated data only. Rule changes that split/merge seed
regions require explicit override rebase/review. It cannot guarantee an old mask's
meaning survives an algorithm change simply because its seed ID remains present.
The input override file survives; semantic compatibility must be reviewed.

Current merge: generated collection -> validated manual replacement. Planned
runtime presentation precedence: defaults -> selected region -> resource/draw
exception -> matching dynamic override -> special-view/fallback policy. Explicit
emissive/material protection and original HUD remain authoritative. No conflicting
overlapping masks or ambiguous priority tie are accepted in this base format.

14. TOOL ARCHITECTURE AND DEBUG OUTPUT

Read-only binary decode -> source inventory -> conservative adjacency -> heuristic
regions -> shared material/fixture rules -> source confidence -> receiver-center
constraints -> light spacing/budget -> neutral profile -> validation -> diagnostics.
It imports the existing resource decompressor and texture-property reader instead
of changing archive/save formats. Source data and input hashes are preserved.

Each level produces JSON plus six SVG maps: inferred regions, independent floor
and ceiling original-light heatmaps, fixture candidates/radius hints, wall material
classification, hazard/state locations. JSON contains all three surface classes,
light source/receiver lists, graph edges, raw event params, unlit/render metadata,
confidence warnings and suppression policies. PNG overviews and the Medical
holdout are generated from those same outputs. Gold radius rings are explicitly
NOT the actual receiving area. Receiver tile masks are in the light records.

No need to open each room in gameplay to inspect inference. Source texture
contact sheets and the whole-level maps were visually inspected in this phase.
They reveal fragmentation, sparse coverage and material contrast, not gameplay
lighting quality, navigation readability, or screen-space light leakage.

Future QA-only GLES overlay design: opt-in build flag + off-by-default marker;
draw tile/region IDs, source confidence colors, receiver masks, source spheres,
disabled fog suggestions and selected material IDs using captured world geometry.
Do not draw radius rings as an occlusion claim. Keep it out of normal touch/HUD
and preserve runtime mode switching. No such mode was implemented here.

17. FALSE POSITIVES, FALSE NEGATIVES AND HUMAN WORK

False positives / risks:
  - Bright signs, controls and TEXTPOLY content can look luminous without casting.
    They remain review-only. A resource assignment can describe an invisible face.
  - Reviewed texture RGB is contaminated by baked illumination/art palette;
    light color is a reasonable seed, not measured emission.
  - Large/corridor/alcove classifications use footprint heuristics, not room names.
    Door cells and lighting boundaries can generate many artificial tiny regions.
  - Whole-texture emissive=true needs a pixel mask; dark material around a bulb
    must not become an unshaded rectangle. Mixed material IDs need reviewed masks.
  - A capped light list limits work, not energy/readability. Four intense nearby
    lights can still flatten contrast; no final-image over-lighting pass is claimed.

False negatives:
  - Sloped luminous ceilings and luminous floors, including the Medical proof.
  - Wall lamp orientation, partial wall exposure and text-bitmap displays.
  - Narrow/diagonal apertures and open-door spill rejected conservatively.
  - Damaged machinery power state, animated warning families, and semantic story
    locations that need source-specific mapping rather than a texture guess.

Human art direction still needed: level identity; signature spaces/reveals;
warm/cold opposition such as the supply alcove; sparse haze placement/density;
important navigation anchors; critical enemy/pickup/control readability;
material emissive masks; unknown/damaged/mixed fixtures; specific plot-state
color changes. One reusable rule or aperture fix should solve repeated cases;
do not answer generator limitations by hand-authoring hundreds of rooms.
